description: Product boundaries for claims and future recognition work.

For UNMULAH, the intended MVP input is Arabic letter-by-letter fingerspelling. Do not describe it as full Saudi Sign Language recognition, continuous sign-language recognition, or Quran translation into sign language. Validate the recognition convention against the selected model and classes in Phase 2.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified the MVP boundaries and asked to avoid overclaiming before model/class validation.

**How to apply:** Use this distinction in product copy and future model evaluation. Do not download or integrate Phase 2 model or dataset candidates during Phase 1.
