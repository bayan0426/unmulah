# Two-minute demo

1. **Home (10s):** “أُنملة: وصول القرآن لكل إنسان” and the read → review → sign → result journey.
2. **Quran browser (10s):** search the 114-surah catalogue; state that only Al-Ikhlas has local Quran text and AI recitation in this MVP.
3. **Reading (10s):** open Al-Ikhlas, point to source attribution, then hide the text.
4. **Camera (15s):** explicitly click تشغيل الكاميرا and point out “المعالجة على جهازك”.
5. **Recognition (30s):** demonstrate a sign, show the candidate, raise the hand to commit, and repeat to build a sequence.
6. **Result (15s):** press إنهاء التسميع and show correct/missing/extra/substituted counts and reference match percentage.
7. **Evidence (10s):** open تفاصيل التعرّف to show raw model output and constrained evidence.
8. **Roadmap and safety (20s):** open الوصول بالإشارة and المصادر. Explain that visual sign assets, Tafsir, full text, and other AI-recitation surahs remain Coming Soon until sources are verified.

Never present placeholder sign cards, the catalogue, or Coming Soon content as implemented Quran-sign interpretation.
