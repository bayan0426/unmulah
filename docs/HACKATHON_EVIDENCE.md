# Hackathon evidence

## AI

The browser runs a real local MLP on 21 MediaPipe hand landmarks and exposes the
raw softmax class/confidence in a collapsed transparency panel.

## Reliability controls

- Selected-target vocabulary constraint retains original softmax probabilities.
- A 9-of-12 rolling agreement rule prevents a single frame from acceptance.
- A candidate requires five consecutive no-hand frames before commit.
- Reference comparison is deterministic dynamic-programming alignment.

## Safety and privacy

- Quran text is not generated by AI.
- Tafsir is not generated by AI.
- Video and landmarks stay in the browser; no face recognition or upload.
- The result is assistive feedback, not a religious ruling or final assessment.

## Accessibility

Arabic RTL screens, responsive layouts, focus styles, text status messages,
large controls, and a sign-first roadmap are included.

## Limits

AI recitation is an Al-Ikhlas MVP. The classifier may make mistakes and requires
specialist and Deaf-user validation. Full official Quran data and legally
reusable sign assets are not yet integrated.
